THE CHALLENGE

A stream can be delivered.
And still disappoint.

Buffering, frozen frames and mismatched audio can interrupt the viewer’s experience. ViewSure brings those visible problems together with timestamped evidence and available network measurements.

Read the project approach +

The proposed hardware-and-software proof of concept captures authorized video and audio without changing the source stream or player. It identifies quality events and reports duration, confidence and supporting evidence.

A configurable experience score explains each deduction. The evaluation plan covers selected devices, resolutions, protocols and controlled network conditions. Measurements that cannot be supported are marked unavailable.

Current stage: concept and implementation planning. No benchmark results are claimed.
